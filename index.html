About us

Dreamy Movies is a small movie-ticket booking site built for our school exhibition. It brings together everything showing this week — sci-fi, comedy, action, drama and more — across four screens, so picking a movie and a showtime takes a minute, not an errand.

Everything runs right in your browser: pick a showtime and a ticket type, fill in your details, and you get an instant confirmation with a reference code. No server, no sign-up. Your tickets stay on the My bookings page whenever you need them again.
